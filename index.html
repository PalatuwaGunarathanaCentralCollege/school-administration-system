Our Historical Journey

Established through the visionary alignment of our founder, Venerable Atampala Gunarathana Thero, the college transformed from a humble village shelter into a leading center of regional learning.


1924

Initial private Buddhist classes start inside a village room thatched with coconut foliage.

March 23, 1927

The institutional layout gets formally recognized and aligned, setting up the framework for formal registrations.

2026

The establishment honors its grand Centenary Jubilee, observing 100 historical years of legacy.
